Billing questions.

How does the 14-day free trial work?
You add a card when you sign up, and the first payment is on day 15. If you cancel before then, with one tap on the Billing page, you pay nothing for the plan. There’s one free trial per business: a business, owner, card, email, phone or address that already had a trial or subscription doesn’t get another.
What does “per location” mean?
Each store, restaurant or site you run is a location. You pay the plan price once per location, with unlimited employees at each one, and manage them all from one dashboard.
Is yearly billing cheaper?
Yes. Yearly is $350 per location for Essential and $650 for Pro, plus tax. That’s two months free compared with paying monthly.
Can I switch plans?
Essential customers can switch to Pro any time from their Billing page.
What is the founding offer?
The first 20 businesses get Pro for $39 CAD per location per month instead of $65, or $390 a year instead of $650, plus tax. It’s applied automatically at checkout while spots remain, and it stays locked in for as long as your subscription is active, including locations you add later. If you cancel, the founding price ends and can’t be restored.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, online from the Billing page. After the free trial, payments aren’t refunded, but you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
Do I have to buy a tablet?
No. Use any recent iPad or Android tablet with a front camera and Wi-Fi. If you want one ready to go, it’s $179 CAD, one-time, plus tax, with an optional lockable wall mount for $69.

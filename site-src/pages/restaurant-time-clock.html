How the time clock works

Restaurant time clock questions.

What’s the best time clock for a restaurant?
One your staff will actually use on a busy shift: fast to punch, hard to cheat, and easy for managers to fix. Sepify Shift runs on a tablet by the door, uses a PIN and a quick face check to stop buddy punching, tracks breaks in one tap and exports hours for payroll.
How do I stop buddy punching in my restaurant?
Make every punch need something only that employee has. In Sepify Shift, that’s their own PIN plus a quick face check on the tablet. Staff who opt out of face check punch in with their PIN and a photo is taken instead.
Can drivers and kitchen staff use the same tablet?
Yes. Everyone at a location punches in on the same tablet with their own PIN. On the Pro plan, you schedule each group by position, such as kitchen, front and drivers.
Does it work for more than one restaurant?
Yes. Each location has its own tablet, and you see all of them from one dashboard on your phone. Billing is per location.

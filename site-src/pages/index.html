Questions owners ask.

What do I need to use Sepify Shift?
A recent iPad or Android tablet with a front camera and Wi-Fi. The time clock runs in the browser, so there’s nothing to install from an app store. If you don’t have a tablet, we can send one ready to go for $179 CAD, one-time, plus tax.
How does the free trial work?
Every plan starts with 14 days free. You add a card when you sign up and the first payment is on day 15. Cancel before then, with one tap on the Billing page, and you pay nothing for the plan. There’s one free trial per business.
What’s the difference between Essential and Pro?
Essential is the time clock: PIN and face check punch-in, breaks, live view, timesheets, manager edits and payroll export. Pro adds scheduling, including Build with AI. You can switch from Essential to Pro any time.
Is face check required? What about privacy?
No. Each employee agrees to face check on the tablet the first time, and anyone who prefers not to can punch in with their PIN and a photo instead. The tablet doesn’t store PINs or face data, and you can turn face check off for your business.
Does Sepify run payroll?
Not yet. Sepify Shift totals each pay period’s hours with breaks taken out, ready to export to the payroll software you already use.
Where is Sepify available?
Across Canada, outside Quebec for now. Prices are in Canadian dollars, plus tax.

Retail time clock questions.

Do I need special hardware for a retail time clock?
No. Any recent iPad or Android tablet with a front camera and Wi-Fi works, because the time clock runs in the browser. If you don’t have one, we can send a tablet ready to go for $179 CAD, one-time, plus tax.
Can staff punch in for each other?
Not easily. Each punch needs the employee’s own PIN and a quick face check. Staff who opt out of face check punch in with their PIN and a photo is taken instead, so you can see who punched.
Can I schedule retail staff too?
Yes, on the Pro plan. Build the week by position, drag shifts to move them, or let Build with AI draft it from your recent weeks. Staff see their shifts on their phone.
How much does it cost per store?
Essential is $35 CAD per location per month and Pro is $65, plus tax, with unlimited employees. Every plan starts with 14 days free.

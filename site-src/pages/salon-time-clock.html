Salon time clock questions.

Where should the tablet go in a salon?
The front desk or the staff room both work. Use a small stand or a wall mount; all it needs is power and Wi-Fi.
Can I schedule stylists with Sepify?
Yes, on the Pro plan. Build the week by position, drag shifts to move them, and staff see their shifts on their phone. Sepify schedules staff shifts, not client appointments.
Does Sepify handle commissions or tips?
No. Sepify Shift tracks hours and breaks and exports them for payroll. Commissions and tips stay in the tools you use today.
How much does it cost?
Essential is $35 CAD per location per month, plus tax, with unlimited employees. Pro adds scheduling for $65. Every plan starts with 14 days free.

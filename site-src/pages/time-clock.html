Time clock questions.

What is buddy punching, and how does Sepify stop it?
Buddy punching is when one employee clocks in or out for someone else. With Sepify Shift, each punch needs the employee’s own PIN and a quick face check on the tablet, so a shared PIN isn’t enough to punch for a friend.
Do employees have to use face check?
No. Each employee is asked to agree on the tablet the first time. Anyone who prefers not to punches in with their PIN, and a photo is taken at punch-in instead. You can also turn face check off for your business.
Does the tablet store PINs or face data?
No. The tablet doesn’t store employees’ PINs or face data.
What happens if the internet goes down?
Punches are saved on the tablet and sent automatically when the connection comes back, so nobody misses a punch.
How do I get hours into payroll?
Each pay period’s hours are totalled per employee with breaks taken out, ready to export for the payroll software you already use. Sepify Shift doesn’t run payroll itself.

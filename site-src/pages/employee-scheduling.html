Scheduling questions.

Which plan includes scheduling?
Scheduling is in the Pro plan: $65 CAD per location per month, or $650 a year, plus tax. Pro includes everything in the time clock too. Essential customers can switch to Pro any time from their Billing page.
How does Build with AI work?
Tap Build with AI and an AI assistant drafts the week from your recent weeks. You can adjust any shift afterwards before staff see it.
Can staff see their schedule on their phone?
Yes. On the Pro plan, staff see their shifts on their phone.
Can I schedule more than one location?
Yes. Every location is managed from one dashboard, and billing is per location.
Is there a free trial for scheduling?
Yes. Pro starts with 14 days free, like every plan. Cancel before day 15 and you pay nothing for the plan.

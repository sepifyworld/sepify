How it works

One shift, start to finish.

Here's what a shift looks like on Sepify Shift. No paper, no punch cards, no adding up hours at the end of the week.

  1. 017:45 AM

    PIN + face check

    Employees enter their PIN, then a quick face check confirms it's really them. No more clocking in for a friend. Green means they're on the clock.

  2. 0212:30 PM

    Breaks in one tap

    Start and end a break on the same tablet. Break time is saved with the shift.

  3. 03Any time

    See who's working

    Check every location from your phone: who's on the clock and who's on break, right now.

  4. 04Payday

    Export for payroll

    Hours are totalled for each person with breaks taken out. Review them and export for payroll.

FAQ

Questions owners ask.

Do I need special hardware?

No. Sepify Shift runs in the browser, so any tablet with Wi-Fi works as the time clock.

What if the internet goes down?

Punches are saved on the tablet and sent automatically when the connection comes back.

How does billing work?

$39 CAD per location per month, or $390 per location per year, plus tax. You pay when you sign up, and you're covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee.

I have more than one location. How does that work?

Each location is billed separately, and you can see all of them from one dashboard.

Is my data private?

Yes. Each business's data is kept separate from every other business.

Where is Sepify Shift available?

In Canada, outside Quebec for now.

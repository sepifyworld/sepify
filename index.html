FAQ

Questions owners ask.

How does Sepify Shift stop buddy punching?

Every punch needs the employee's own PIN plus a quick face check on the tablet, so a coworker can't clock in for them. Face check is optional for each employee: they agree first, and anyone who prefers not to punches in with their PIN and a photo instead.

What if an employee types the wrong PIN?

For employees who use face check, the face has to match the person whose PIN was entered. If it doesn't, the punch isn't recorded, so hours never end up under the wrong name.

Is Sepify Shift a good time clock for restaurants?

It was built in one. Our founder runs restaurants on it every day: kitchen and front-of-house staff punch in on one tablet, breaks are tracked, and every location shows up on one dashboard. It works for cafés, bakeries, retail and other shift teams too.

Does Sepify Shift do employee scheduling?

Yes. Build each week's schedule by position, drag shifts to move them, or let the AI assistant draft the week for you from your recent weeks. Then adjust anything you like.

Do I need special hardware?

No. Sepify Shift runs in the browser, so a recent Android tablet or iPad with a front camera and Wi-Fi works as the time clock. Don't have one? We can send a ready-to-go tablet for $179, plus an optional $69 wall mount.

What if the internet goes down?

Punches are saved on the tablet and sent automatically when the connection comes back.

How does billing work?

$39 CAD per location per month, or $390 per location per year, plus tax. You pay when you sign up, and your first subscription is covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee, once per business (terms apply).

I have more than one location. How does that work?

Each location is billed separately, and you can see all of them from one dashboard.

Is my data private?

Yes. Each business's data is kept separate from every other business.

Where is Sepify Shift available?

In Canada, outside Quebec for now.

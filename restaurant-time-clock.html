FAQ

Restaurant time clock questions.

What is the best time clock app for a restaurant?

Look for one that stops buddy punching, tracks breaks, keeps working when the internet drops, and shows every location in one place. Sepify Shift does all four on a tablet you already own, from $35 CAD per location per month.

How do restaurants stop buddy punching?

By making every punch prove who it is. With Sepify Shift, each employee enters their own PIN and then a quick face check runs on the tablet. Employees who prefer not to use face check punch in with their PIN and a photo instead.

Can my staff clock in on a tablet?

Yes. Sepify Shift runs in the browser on a recent Android tablet or iPad with a front camera and Wi-Fi. Mount it near the door or the kitchen, and staff punch in there. We can also send a ready-to-go tablet for $179 CAD.

Does it track breaks?

Yes. Staff start and end breaks with one tap on the tablet. Break time is saved with each shift and taken out of paid hours in the timesheet.

What happens if the restaurant's internet goes down?

Punches are saved on the tablet and sent automatically when the connection comes back, so nobody's hours are lost.

Does Sepify Shift do restaurant scheduling?

Yes, on the Pro plan. Build each week's schedule by position (kitchen, front, drivers), drag shifts to move them, or let the AI assistant draft the week for you from your recent weeks. Then adjust anything you like.

Where is Sepify Shift available?

In Canada, outside Quebec for now.
